About Me

Crafting interfaces that speak every language

I'm a Senior Frontend Developer with 10+ years of experience building scalable, high-performance web applications for international projects across the USA, Portugal, Germany, and the Gulf region — especially Saudi Arabia. My work spans government, fintech, education, real estate, and gaming, including full RTL/Arabic interfaces, complex dashboards, and real-time features for multicultural, cross-functional teams.

I also help startups build production-ready web applications from scratch — choosing the right technology stack, defining frontend architecture, setting development standards and workflows, building web teams, hiring junior-to-senior developers, and mentoring engineers as ideas turn into maintainable products.

Beyond code, I write technical articles on Medium, mentor junior developers, and stay current with the frontend ecosystem. I speak Arabic (native), English (fluent), and German (A2).
